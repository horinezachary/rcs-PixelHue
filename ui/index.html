HOW IT WORKS

A preset key loads to preview at once. With Load to PGM it goes straight to program, after a second press within 3 seconds. Take and Cut act on the screens selected on the switcher, which loading a preset selects. Everything shown is read from the switcher about once a second.

Named saves hold the switcher list, Allow controls and the transition time. Restoring one never sends anything to a switcher. The port is 19998 unless set in the web view.
